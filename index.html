HOW IT WORKS

Four phases per round

01
Memorize

A position flashes on the board for a few seconds. Study it.

02

The board is cleared. Use the piece palette to place every piece back where you remember it.

03
Check

Press Check Position. Every square is validated — correct, wrong, missing, or extra.

04
Learn

The original position returns with your mistakes highlighted. Difficulty adapts. Next round.
